# Code of Conduct

## Our standard

We aim for a respectful, evidence-based, privacy-conscious project space. Participants must:

- discuss ideas and evidence in good faith;
- welcome good-faith questions and corrections;
- protect private, sensitive, and security-relevant information;
- avoid harassment, discrimination, threats, and personal attacks;
- avoid posting credentials, real vault contents, personal chats, or other private data.

## Enforcement

Report conduct concerns privately through the repository's [security advisory contact](https://github.com/Zannel12/agent-recall/security/advisories/new). Do not include secrets or private data. Maintainers may remove content, reject contributions, or restrict participation when this standard is violated.

This is a project-specific policy, not a copied external code of conduct.
